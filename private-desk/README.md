# Private FX research desk

Start locally with `powershell -File private-desk/start.ps1` from the repository root. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765. The generated login password is in `.private-fx/access.txt`; keep that local file private. The listener binds only to loopback and needs no database or Docker. Stop it with `powershell -File private-desk/stop.ps1`.

From `pipeline`, run `python -m src.private_desk.cli research` to capture sources and rebuild research, then `python -m src.private_desk.cli verify`. Refresh on the dashboard does the same work in the background and retains the prior report after failure. Reports and raw captures are under the ignored `.private-fx` directory; each run retains a separate report. CSV exports include trade outcomes and assumptions for Excel review.

From `pipeline`, run `python -m src.private_desk.cli recalculate` to create a new quantitative report from the saved, hash-checked daily captures without a network or AI call. This preserves earlier versioned reports and source/reference/session evidence. The revised method conditions probability and current qualification on policy, BUY/SELL direction and strength bucket. Old AI text is archived and explicitly detached from the new calculations. The separate overlap replay allows at most two planned positions with no shared currency, uses full holding horizons to reserve capacity and reports realised research R rather than account returns. CSV exports include accepted/skipped replay decisions. See `docs/PRIVATE_CALIBRATION_PORTFOLIO_CONTRACT.md` and `reports/private-calibration-acceptance.md`; zero current trades remain qualified.

Run `python -m src.private_desk.cli review` from `pipeline` to refresh current policy observations and headlines and request one bounded AI review while preserving every saved historical result and candidate. `review --no-ai` records an unavailable AI result without a provider call. Headlines must have witnessed dates within fourteen calendar days and UTC capture receipts within forty-eight hours. The exact input message and accepted structured output are saved separately with hashes, along with the pinned parent report. The dashboard shows the review time, publisher coverage gaps and conditional scenarios. Publication freshness does not prove economic relevance; RBNZ news remains unavailable until a repeatable adapter is verified. This command does not refresh prices or grant trade eligibility.

Run `python -m src.private_desk.cli replay` to recompute the saved daily, reference-price, session, bid/ask, historical-vintage, release-clock, policy-history and overlap audits from their pinned captures. This makes no network or AI call and compares the results against the saved report. It also checks current-review inputs, captured output, pair projections and preservation against the parent. AI commentary remains a saved current review rather than a deterministic forecast.

From `pipeline`, `python -m src.private_desk.cli bidask --week 2025-W40` captures the seven private pairs for that named ISO week, audits the original minute archives and attaches a separate evidence panel to the saved report. Repeat `--week` for up to twelve distinct weeks; `--pair EURUSD` restricts the universe; `--refresh` makes new captures while retaining old bodies and receipts. Cache reuse checks original hashes. It never silently fills gaps, repairs quotes or turns indicative archive differences into broker costs. Dashboard refresh only reads the saved archive evidence. See `reports/private-bidask-acceptance.md` for actual coverage and the rejected CHF sample. Timestamp meaning and execution qualification remain open.

From `pipeline`, `python -m src.private_desk.cli vintage --series CPIAUCSL --vintage 2023-02-13 --vintage 2023-02-14 --vintage 2024-02-13 --observation-start 2022-01-01 --observation-end 2023-01-01` captures those named ALFRED snapshots. The initial registry allows CPIAUCSL and DGS2, both USD series. Repeated series and vintage arguments form a cross-product of at most twelve distinct requests; choose a separate observation window for each invocation. Original responses and receipts are retained, cache reuse checks hashes, and offline replay recomputes revisions. A wrong series/vintage header or conflicting observation fails the audit. The historical-fundamentals panel shows missing currencies, unknown release times and unqualified metadata. Dashboard refresh only reads this saved evidence. No historical macro feature or forecast is added. See `reports/private-vintage-acceptance.md` for the four actual captures and remaining source requirements.

Scope: seven USD majors—EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF and USDJPY—plus the retained EURGBP research cross. JPY is included under the owner's private-desk amendment. Holdings are three or five business days in the initial trials. Half exits at +1R; the remaining half moves its stop to entry and targets +3R. Full target profit is +2R gross. Costs and adverse bar-order ambiguity are included. There is no guaranteed +1R or calibrated 60–70% win rate.

Evidence views are separate: long official reference-close direction research; daily reconstructed OHLC stop/target research; recent hourly session comparisons; and a current fundamental/headline review. Malformed daily rows are quarantined without changing prices. Features and complete holding windows crossing a gap are excluded. Reference closes cannot prove a stop or target fill. AI current news does not enter historical backtests. Unknown probabilities and unqualified fundamental legs stay visible. Every candidate requires independent technical analysis and manual execution. No broker integration exists.

From `pipeline`, `python -m src.private_desk.cli release --release-source FOMC --release-date 2023-02-01 --release-date 2023-03-22 --release-date 2023-07-26` captures those named official statements. `--release-source CPI` selects BLS archive requests. At most twelve distinct dates are allowed; `--refresh` preserves a new original and receipt. Wrong dates, titles, clocks, timezone labels, target arithmetic or measure identities fail closed. The separate panel shows source-stated EST/EDT clocks, UTC conversion and original announced ranges. A source clock is not actual historical receipt, an effective rate, a two-year yield spread or a surprise. Blocked responses remain visible. Existing forecasts and backtests are unchanged; dashboard refresh only reads saved release evidence. See `reports/private-release-acceptance.md` for the actual three FOMC successes and three blocked CPI captures.

DeepSeek runs through the central pipeline AI client. `PRIVATE_FX_AI_ENV_FILE` or ignored `.private-fx/provider.json` can point to an owner-authorized server-side environment file. API keys are never sent to the browser. One bounded call reviews the eight-pair report; its predictive value remains unvalidated.

The AI supplies conditional headline scenarios. It cannot restate numeric rates, bounds, differentials or carry in its prose; those facts remain in the captured policy table. Structural checks cannot prove semantic accuracy or full-article grounding. Current headline context is excluded from historical results. Actual results and five remaining research workstreams are in `reports/private-desk-acceptance.md`.

The local password-cookie service is for this workstation. Remote deployment needs TLS, durable identity, protected data storage and a separate release review. Existing public research and immutable ledgers retain their historical contracts.

The optional Jev adapter runs separately from dashboard research. It uses replay-verified public article passages, saves a deterministic shortlist and requests narrow typed evidence judgments only when explicitly enabled. From `pipeline`, this actual-source example creates a disabled shadow receipt with no provider call:

```powershell
python -m src.private_desk.jev_shadow evaluate --evidence "D:\Projects\fxregimelab\.private-fx\evidence\receipts\9115290228a62c4505554ca84964a65a.json" --currency USD --claim "The Committee decided to raise the target range for the federal funds rate by 1/4 percentage point." --mechanism "A policy-rate increase is explicitly stated."
python -m src.private_desk.jev_shadow replay
```

Default evidence and shadow roots are `.private-fx/evidence` and `.private-fx/jev-shadow` at the repository root. Adding `--enable` permits one bounded request through the central AI client only if `TYPESAFE_API_KEY` exists in the process. Missing keys and provider failures retain indeterminate support. The model is pinned to `jev-1.13.0`; live compatibility, quality, latency and billed cost remain untested. No TypeSafe SDK or live key is configured in this packet. The example document is retrospective, and its publication value is a supplied feed claim rather than verified historical availability. Six actual captures replay; two blocked downloads remain recorded. All shadow judgments require review and remain separate from probabilities, eligibility and the UI. See `reports/private-jev-shadow-acceptance.md` for direct checks and the remaining labelled benchmark.

From `pipeline`, run `python -m src.private_desk.cli policy-history --policy-start 1999-01-05 --policy-end 2026-09-30` to capture the eight named BIS policy histories, including Japanese policy. Repeat `--currency USD` and other supported currency flags to restrict a request. `--refresh` retains new originals and receipts. The window begins no earlier than 5 January 1999 and spans at most 31 years; each response is bounded to 8 MiB. Exact compilation/source definitions and units must match the reviewed source definitions. Missing M/NaN rows stay missing. Base-minus-quote marker differences and five-weekday changes require exact observations; a change across an instrument definition is unavailable. The authenticated panel shows actual dates, source counts, missing values and unqualified feature coverage. These retrospective snapshots do not enter forecasts, AI inputs or price calibration. `replay` reproduces all histories and features from the pinned originals and preserves the earlier seven-leg method. The browser receives source summaries and six recent feature rows; the full saved report remains intact. See `reports/private-jpy-policy-acceptance.md` and `docs/PRIVATE_POLICY_HISTORY_CONTRACT.md`.

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/#decisionJournal` for every published suggestion and abstention. Each original observation freezes its reasons, sources, clock and diagnostic plans. Later outcome evaluations append separately; repeated displays do not become new signals.
