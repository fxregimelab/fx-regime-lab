Revision 1, 2 October 2026 IST. This implements the owner's revised goal: help confirm or challenge technical-analysis views across seven USD majors and EURGBP over two to five business days. The owner chooses entries, stops, exits and position management. Preserve every old study, source and decision. Do not loosen data-integrity or source-age controls.

## Meaning and first method

Bias context is descriptive evidence, separate from trade qualification. Price phase means observed trend, range or transition. A continuation watch means agreement in past price moves; a reversal watch means conflict worth investigating. Neither is a continuation/reversal probability. Source-backed fundamentals may support, contradict or leave a thesis unresolved. A policy-rate level alone does not determine FX direction.

Register a fixed first method, private-bias-context-v1, before inspecting its current results. Use only hash-verified original daily prices completed before their capture's UTC date and at or before an explicit observation-date cutoff. Require exact consecutive weekday observations separately for each metric; reject corrupt identities, invalid prices and missing windows. The first bar's gap marker describes the preceding unused interval. A valid short metric may remain visible when a longer metric is unavailable.

Report five- and twenty-weekday close changes, requiring six and 21 closes respectively, ending exactly at the frozen cutoff. For a complete 21-observation window calculate path efficiency as absolute first-to-last close change divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes; a flat path has efficiency zero. Range position uses the last 20 bars of that 21-observation window (positions 2 through 21), their highest high and lowest low. A zero range has no range-position value. These are observed quantities, not predicted returns. A valid 21-bar window necessarily contains the valid six-bar window; a longer valid window with an invalid short window must never be manufactured.

With complete five- and twenty-day moves, aligned nonzero signs and efficiency at least 0.30 describe TREND_UP or TREND_DOWN and a continuation watch. Opposing signs describe TRANSITION and a reversal watch, with MIXED bias. Other complete cases describe RANGE/MIXED. If only the five-day window is valid, show SHORT_TERM_ONLY and its observed bullish/bearish/mixed context; leave the full price phase unavailable. Never infer accumulation/distribution, a Wyckoff A-E phase or institutional order flow from this checklist. Set volume confirmation to UNAVAILABLE_TOTAL_OTC_VOLUME. This fixed heuristic is not optimized or validated as a forecast.

No minimum trade count, selected-policy profitability, 60% net-profit threshold, fixed bracket or broker fill is required to DISPLAY descriptive context. Calibrated probability, forecast expectancy, fundamental directional confirmation and power-law qualification remain null/unavailable unless separately verified. Show existing dated policy facts, source limitations and conditional AI scenarios beside the price view; do not convert a higher policy rate into automatic support. Prior detached AI cannot become current confirmation.

## Positive skew and further research

The objective is useful directional evidence and, separately, positive expectancy with a meaningful positive tail under the owner's execution policy. Positive skew, heavy tails and a power law are different claims. Required future metrics include median and mean returns, loss-tail quantiles, profit concentration in the largest winners, downside excursions, drawdown and uncertainty; actual discretionary results require actual execution records. Do not fit a power law to the current fixed-exit sample or use a high win-rate threshold as a substitute for these measures.

Independent forward evaluation of phase/continuation/reversal labels and historically available fundamental surprises remains open. Current archive data are already examined development evidence. New probabilistic or strategy trials need their own frozen event definitions, chronological evaluation and selection controls.
