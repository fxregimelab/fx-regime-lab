The operating target is 3–5 new trades per ISO week, maximum five open positions, maximum two positions within one correlated directional risk group. The minimum is a measured target and not an order quota. Count zero-entry weeks over the measured cohort, weekly shortfall, oversubscribed opportunities, rejected reasons, accepted calls, missing forecasts and cost-adjusted outcomes. Distinguish all historical research calls from qualified recommendations. Unqualified price forecasts cannot be promoted just to satisfy frequency.

Keep the original two-position/no-shared-currency overlap replay unchanged for legacy comparisons. Add a separate policy with five concurrent positions and five entries per week. Rank using only forecasts available at the signal, then a fixed pair order; never use realised returns or whole-history pair performance. Reserve capacity through the full planned horizon; do not use early exits to choose another trade retrospectively. Duplicate simultaneous exposure to one pair is rejected.

Correlation means correlation of exact-date daily log returns, not price levels. Use only observations before the signal date; exclude returns across gaps or invalid rows. Require at least 40 matched observations in a 60-observation rolling window and a recent final matched observation. Unknown, constant or insufficient correlation holds simultaneous admission rather than assuming independence. Multiply pair correlation by both trade directions to measure shared profit/loss risk. Connect positions with signed correlation at least 0.70; reject an admission if its connected component would exceed two. The threshold and window are fixed research choices, not optimized profit claims. Show currency long/short counts separately; equal stop-risk units do not mean equal currency notional.

Measure both the short-window correlation and a longer descriptive window where supported; test sign changes and stress-era performance. These are diagnostics, not a guarantee against joint losses. A portfolio view needs realised-close drawdown explicitly separated from account drawdown and unrealised risk.

Hedge research is separate from admission: predeclared opposite exposure, lagged ratio, full two-leg paths, spreads/slippage/financing, downside and net-return comparisons against the unhedged position. Merely reversing a position or adding a negatively correlated leg does not establish higher expected profit. Do not execute or qualify an automatic hedge in this packet.
