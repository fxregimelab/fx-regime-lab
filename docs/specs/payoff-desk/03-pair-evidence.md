# PD pair evidence and strategy hypotheses

These are hypotheses to validate, not current market calls or permanent pair
personalities. No current rate, policy forecast or intervention threshold is
inferred from old repository prose.

## EURUSD

Compare Fed/ECB expected paths and *changes* in relative pricing, relative
growth/inflation surprises, euro-area fragmentation/energy exposure, positioning
and liquidity. A strong release can disappoint stronger expectations. Distinguish
observed yield moves from inferred policy pricing; a two-year yield is not a
pure policy expectation. DXY contains a large euro component: it is not an
independent confirmation of EURUSD. Deduplicate mechanically related evidence.

Proposed hypotheses: policy-repricing continuation after a documented surprise;
trend pullback when relative rates and price agree; exhaustion/reversal only
when positioning and a catalyst support it. Each needs a registered entry rule,
cutoff, horizon, invalidation and comparable no-macro baseline.

## USDJPY

Compare US/Japan expected rates, carry/financing, Japanese inflation/wages and
policy communication, risk deleveraging and official intervention risk. Japan's
Ministry of Finance determines intervention; the Bank of Japan acts as agent.
Do not equate a fixed spot level with a known intervention probability. Japan
has intervened in different directions historically; the old identity sentence
about tolerating JPY weakness and responding to JPY strength is not a validated
operational rule. Flag it for owner review; do not edit IDENTITY.md here.

Proposed hypotheses: carry continuation, rate-repricing continuation, documented
carry-unwind conditions, and intervention/event dislocation abstention. A setup
name shared with EURUSD does not prove identical parameter values or drivers.
Separate pair estimates require adequate evidence; a small sample is UNKNOWN,
not permission to fit a large bespoke rule set.

## Data contract

For every source retain publisher, URL/feed ID, licence/use permissions,
publication time, first usable availability, retrieval time, reference period,
revision/vintage, timezone and immutable snapshot hash. Decision cutoff must
follow availability. Store actual and pre-release consensus separately; official
release text alone does not establish the consensus. No retrospective consensus
reconstruction from revised pages. Syndication counts as one evidence family.

Weekly COT is lagged context, not live institutional flow. Broker tick volume
is not signed interdealer order flow. ETF/equity/oil/gold/yield observations can
be tested as context; none is automatically an independent or stable causal
predictor. Futures positioning proxies need coverage/basis limitations stated.
Authoritative calendar/news entitlement remains BLOCKED until verified; public
research reading does not provision a production feed.

## Confidence and abstention

Define event-specific forecasts separately: hit 1R before stop, full target before
stop, net positive settlement, and directional price change at the fixed horizon.
An AI adjective/checklist grade is not a probability. Evaluate Brier/reliability
and sample sizes only against matching outcomes. Forecasts are optional until
calibration exists. Unknown required context, expired catalyst, source conflict,
unexplained repricing or impossible payoff geometry means WAIT.

## Target selection

Use a measurable target mechanism (observed structural level or preregistered
volatility/horizon rule), then compute blended net R. If plausible movement
within <=T+5 cannot support >=3R net, abstain rather than stretching a chart
target. The preferred horizon is today/next 1–2 days; the existing T+5 ceiling
is unchanged. More potential RR alone does not improve expectancy.

## Expansion

Gold first remains a proposed contextual input with its own source/ablation
test. Seven-major execution and gold execution are outside this scope. Shared
USD exposure is a portfolio factor even across different pairs. New pairs need
the existing public expansion criteria and separate owner approval.
