# Direction-specific calibration and overlapping-call replay

Revision 2, 1 October 2026. Independent review tightened current qualification to the exact held-out policy-direction-strength cell. This advances R4 in the private desk goal. It does not close R1–R5 or the public repair register.

The fixed twelve price trials and their training-only selection remain. A probability now requires the selected policy, the signal's BUY or SELL direction, and its predeclared strength bucket. At least forty matured calibration trades in that direction and twenty in that direction's bucket are required. The twenty count is the exact joint cell after filtering the selected policy and direction. The comparison forecast uses that direction's training outcomes. The current candidate's held-out probability check and positive-return check require its exact policy, direction and strength bucket, including at least one hundred matured predictions in that cell. Profits from another direction or strength bucket cannot qualify it. Brier comparison and ECE limit remain. These are research gates, not a promise of future accuracy. Approximate intervals still do not resolve dependence or selection risk.

The report identifies the new calculation method. Existing versioned reports remain intact. Recalculation reads their pinned captures without fetching prices, news or AI. Old AI text must be visibly detached from revised quantitative inputs; it is not regenerated or described as a current review.

The portfolio audit is a fixed counterfactual over held-out price calls, including calls without a qualified probability. It is not a reconstruction of an approved trading strategy. Calls enter at the recorded next-bar open. At most two positions may be open, one research risk unit per position. A currency may appear in only one open position, regardless of direction; this conservatively limits common USD and EUR/GBP exposure without claiming to estimate correlations. There is no leverage or account capital assumption.

Order simultaneous calls by their existing fold-time probability, highest first, unavailable forecasts last; break ties by the fixed seven-pair order. Never rank by realised return or later qualification. Capacity and currency occupancy use each accepted call's full declared holding horizon. A position releases capacity only on a later date, even if a recorded stop exited earlier; uncertain intraday ordering cannot free a same-day slot. Reject duplicate identities, unknown pairs, non-canonical daily dates, missing or contradictory full-horizon boundaries, invalid forecasts and non-finite outcomes. Do not silently drop malformed calls.

Show accepted and skipped calls, reasons, exposures and the close-date realised R ledger. Simultaneous closures are summed before calculating the realised curve. Its drawdown excludes unrealised losses and is not an account equity drawdown. Show the worst observed call and mean of the worst ceiling(5% × accepted count) calls as sample descriptions, not future tail guarantees. The protected CSV includes replay selection and skipped-call reasons for all original calls, each explicitly unqualified. Include a zero-return positive control for ambiguity in same-date exits, collision controls, and a test showing changed future outcomes cannot change prior selection. Keep source-cost and price-quality limits visible. No new cost, spread or broker-fill evidence is inferred.

The authenticated dashboard must show the probability conditioning and portfolio audit, including failures and missing data. Offline replay must reproduce the new backtests and portfolio using pinned captures. The source audits, session/reference studies, news and prior reports remain unchanged.
