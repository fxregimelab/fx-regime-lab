## Experiment and promotion rules

Start with a source-quality experiment, not a strategy optimization. Collect at least 240 authentic source-event groups and two independently produced human label sets, then adjudicate disagreements. No such corpus or human labels exist in this packet. AI agreement cannot stand in for these labels. Keep shared events, duplicates and their passages in the same group. Freeze the earliest 60% for development, next 20% for threshold selection and latest 20% for the final test, with five business days between periods. Thin pair/event/no-match strata remain inconclusive. Reusing the final period after prompt changes requires a new untouched test.

Compare with BM25/rules, exact quote matching and deterministic span heuristics on the same candidates. Predeclare Recall@5 gain of at least ten percentage points with a grouped-bootstrap lower confidence bound above zero; macro-F1 at least 0.80; no-match and citation-error recall at least 0.90; 100% byte-linked selected spans; and no tested hostile-text bypass. Report all classes, contradictions, abstentions, coverage and failures. These are proposed pilot targets, not observed results or a sufficient trading sample.

For this small shadow pilot, set a provisional five-dollar request ceiling, a five-second p95 latency target, and the twelve-second timeout above. These are independent design decisions for source review, not vendor performance claims. Before live calls, confirm the account-visible terms and conservative projected cost within the ceiling; record actual billed cost when available and keep it unknown otherwise. Crossing the ceiling or missing the quality/latency gate prevents promotion. The deterministic fallback remains available without Jev.

Passing these gates permits only a source-review aid. Any later forecast feature needs authentic point-in-time sources, expectations and observed surprises where claimed, a predeclared economic hypothesis, grouped chronological evaluation, overlap controls, calibration and net-after-cost comparison with the frozen price model. The current 2–5-day policy and +1R partial/+3R remainder rules remain unchanged. The economic question is whether the information is unexpected and has an incremental relation to future net returns; a well-labelled headline alone does not answer it.
